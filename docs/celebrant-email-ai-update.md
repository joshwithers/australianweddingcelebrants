## How couples are finding celebrants now

Alongside conventional search, a couple can ask an assistant in their phone,
browser or ChatGPT for help. Something like:

The directory used to rely mainly on ordinary web pages. We have now published

## What we did

Your public profile on australianweddingcelebrants.com.au now has a direct
Markdown companion and appears in the directory's machine-readable indexes.
Compatible tools can use that public information to answer a couple's question and
link back to your canonical profile.

Think of it as giving those tools a clean, current index rather than asking them to
interpret a visual web page from scratch.

## The new enquiry channel

We also built something called an "enquiry relay". Here's how it works in plain English:

1. A couple asks their AI assistant to help them find a celebrant.
2. The assistant shortlists a few celebrants from this directory — you might be one of them.
3. If the couple asks the assistant to get in touch with you, it sends us a proper enquiry — the couple's names, their email, the wedding date, the location, and a bit about what they're after.
4. We forward that enquiry straight to your email.
5. You reply directly to the couple. We're not in the middle.

**No commission. No contract. No fees.** We don't clip the ticket if you book the wedding. The enquiry relay is a free extra, and it only operates after you explicitly opt in.

## What you need to know

- **It is not on by default.** Your public listing can be discovered by compatible AI assistants, but the relay cannot email you unless your stored profile has explicit consent enabled.

- **You choose whether to enable it.** Log in to edit your listing and check "Let AI agents relay wedding enquiries to my email address". You can uncheck it again at any time. No offence taken if this isn't for you.

- **You can block a sender.** Every enquiry email has a "Report spam" link at the bottom. Click it once and that sender is blocked for 30 days.

- **Limits are in place.** Nobody can spam you. Max one enquiry per sender per celebrant per day, and a ceiling of 30 enquiries per celebrant per day across all senders.

- **You'll get a summary.** Once a week, we'll email you a short digest of any activity so you know what's going on, without you needing to count inbox messages.

## Why we did this

Couples planning weddings in 2026 use different tools than couples planning weddings in 2015. If the directory doesn't keep up, you lose. Doing nothing wasn't an option.

None of this costs you anything or requires you to change how you work. Your public profile is already available to compatible search and AI tools. If you separately opt in to the enquiry relay, you reply directly to any enquiries that land in your inbox — exactly like you already do.
