Lucy Roberts is an award-winning, Commonwealth-authorised marriage celebrant and MC based on the Gold Coast, dedicated to creating ceremonies that feel authentically, uniquely *you*. Recognised as the #1 Celebrant for 2023, 2024 and 2025 in The Wedding Industry Awards, Lucy brings warmth, professionalism and a light-hearted touch to every celebration she conducts.

**Your ceremony, your way**

Lucy believes your wedding ceremony should be a true reflection of who you are as a couple. Whether you're envisioning something heartfelt and romantic, modern and light-hearted, short and simple, or completely unconventional, she works closely with you from the very first conversation to craft a ceremony that genuinely captures your relationship, personalities and love story. Every element—from the words spoken to the moments celebrated—is personalised to ensure your day feels authentically yours. The greatest honour, Lucy says, is being trusted to play such an important role in a couple's most significant day. That responsibility drives her commitment to understanding what matters most to you, listening carefully to your vision, and bringing it to life in a way that feels natural, meaningful and stress-free.

**Comprehensive celebrant services**

As a full-service celebrant, Lucy conducts a range of ceremonies beyond traditional weddings. She specialises in marriage ceremonies featuring your unique love story, personalised vows, and all the elements you choose to include. She also conducts civil ceremonies for couples seeking a secular celebration, celebration of life events honouring those we've loved, and MC services that bring energy, warmth and professionalism to your reception, ensuring your special moments are shared seamlessly with your guests. Each ceremony is crafted with the same dedication to personal meaning and authentic celebration, whether it's a full wedding day or an intimate gathering.

**Experience and expertise you can trust**

With over 25 years of experience behind the scenes at weddings and events, Lucy understands how all the little details work together to create an unforgettable celebration. She knows what it takes to feel confident on your wedding day, and she's committed to supporting you every step of the way—from navigating the legal requirements to managing the logistics that keep everything running smoothly. On the day itself, Lucy brings a calm, professional presence that allows you to relax and truly savour the moment. She guides the ceremony naturally, handles the formalities seamlessly, and creates space for genuine emotion and joy to shine through. Her warmth and light-hearted touch ensure that even the most formal moments feel personal and connected.

**Your journey, supported from start to finish**

Lucy works with couples throughout the entire process, taking care of all the Commonwealth requirements so you don't have to worry. She listens carefully to your story, asks thoughtful questions, and collaborates with you to design a ceremony that feels right. Whether you need guidance on ceremony structure, help with wording, or reassurance that everything will run smoothly, she's there to support you with both expertise and genuine care. She's also experienced in conducting video ceremonies, bringing the same level of personalisation and professionalism to remote celebrations.

**Serving the Gold Coast, Scenic Rim and beyond**

Based on the Gold Coast, Lucy conducts ceremonies throughout the Gold Coast, Scenic Rim, Mount Tamborine, Brisbane and Northern New South Wales—from the Tweed Coast to Byron Bay and beyond. Whether you're planning an intimate beachside celebration, a garden ceremony in the hinterland, or a Brisbane city wedding, she brings the same level of personalisation and professionalism to every location.

Your wedding should feel authentic, stress-free and completely yours—because it's your day, your way. Lucy is here to ensure it's everything you've imagined and more.
