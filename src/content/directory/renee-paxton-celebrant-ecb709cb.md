Renée Paxton is a Melbourne-based celebrant who brings warmth, authenticity, and genuine joy to every ceremony she creates. With a philosophy that love should be celebrated exactly as it is—without pretence or cookie-cutter scripts—Renée has built a reputation for delivering ceremonies that feel deeply personal and wonderfully memorable.

Renée's approach sits at the sweet spot between cheeky and heartfelt, playful and profound. She believes ceremonies should feel like you: full of personality, full of love, and absolutely never like a formal script delivered by a stranger. Whether you're envisioning something romantic and soulful, hilarious and irreverent, wildly unconventional, or a blend of everything, Renée works closely with couples to create something that's authentically, unmistakably theirs.

As both a Commonwealth authorised marriage celebrant and experienced MC, Renée's services extend far beyond weddings. She specialises in elopements for couples seeking intimacy and simplicity, vow renewals for those celebrating milestones, MC duties for receptions that need energy and flow, funerals and celebrations of life, and any meaningful moment worth honouring. If it matters to you, it matters to her.

Renée's process is thoughtful and collaborative. She begins by genuinely getting to know you—your story, your values, your sense of humour, what makes your relationship unique. From there, she carefully plans and writes a completely bespoke ceremony that captures your voice and vision. On the day, she delivers with confidence and care, handling all the technical and legal requirements so you can simply turn up and be present with the people you love. She'll lodge all the paperwork to ensure everything is official and legally binding.

Her style brings the romance, holds the room, keeps things flowing seamlessly, and lands the meaningful moments exactly where they belong. She's equally comfortable bringing genuine laughter to a room full of guests or creating an intimate moment of quiet emotion. Couples appreciate her ability to balance professionalism with personality, ensuring their ceremony feels both polished and perfectly imperfect.

A fierce LGBTQIA+ ally, Renée celebrates love in all its forms. She's passionate about creating space where every couple feels completely welcome and seen, regardless of who they love or how they choose to celebrate.

Based in Naarm (Melbourne), Renée travels throughout Australia, making her available for celebrations anywhere across the country. Whether you're planning a huge wedding celebration, a small and intimate gathering, a themed fantasy wedding, or something beautifully unconventional, she's genuinely excited to be part of your story.

If you're looking for a celebrant who can bring authenticity, warmth, and real expertise to your ceremony—someone who'll make people laugh, honour the big feelings, and create something truly unforgettable—Renée would be honoured to help you celebrate.
