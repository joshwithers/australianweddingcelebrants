Rohit is the founder of Star Celebrant, an award-winning marriage celebrant based in Bella Vista in Sydney's Hills District. With over 200 ceremonies celebrated across Sydney and surrounds, and holding the prestigious 2026 Quality Business Award for Best Marriage Celebrant in The Hills Shire, Rohit has earned the trust and five-star reviews of couples from diverse cultural backgrounds.

At the heart of Rohit's practice is a simple belief: your ceremony should feel genuinely yours. He specialises in creating deeply personal celebrations that honour exactly who you are as a couple—your story, your values, and your traditions. Whether you're blending two cultures, two families, or two worlds entirely, Rohit brings warmth, sensitivity, expertise, and genuine joy to every ceremony he conducts.

Rohit is a bilingual and interfaith specialist, conducting ceremonies in English, Hindi, and Punjabi. This multilingual capability makes him uniquely positioned to work with couples celebrating Indian weddings—including Hindu, Sikh, and Punjabi ceremonies—as well as multicultural partnerships where cultural sensitivity and authenticity matter most. He understands that no two love stories are ever the same, and his approach reflects that understanding.

His style is warm, unhurried, and deeply personal. Rather than delivering a generic script, Rohit takes considerable time to get to know both of you before your wedding day. He listens to your story, learns what matters most to you both, and crafts ceremony words that feel written just for you—because they are. Couples consistently praise how his ceremonies move everyone in the room, transforming not just the legal moment but the emotional heart of the day.

Star Celebrant offers a full range of ceremony styles to suit your vision. From intimate 15-minute legal ceremonies to full cultural celebrations, from elopements to grand affairs, Rohit works with you to design a day that fits your needs. He's experienced in conducting weddings across all venues throughout Sydney and the Hills District, and also offers ceremonies at his own peaceful, private garden setting—The Garden Sanctuary at Bella Vista—a serene space perfect for intimate weddings with no venue hire fees.

With 84+ five-star Google reviews, Rohit's couples speak to the care and craft he brings to every celebration. Guests frequently comment on how personal, engaging, and heartfelt the ceremonies feel. Whether you're planning a small, meaningful gathering or a larger multicultural celebration, you can expect a celebrant who listens, understands, and delivers a ceremony that truly reflects who you are.

Rohit serves the Hills District, Blacktown, Parramatta, Sydney CBD, Bella Vista, and Greater Western Sydney. If you're looking for a Commonwealth authorised marriage celebrant who specialises in multicultural weddings, Indian ceremonies, or intimate celebrations, Star Celebrant is ready to help you plan your day.
