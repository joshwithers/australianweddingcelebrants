Clare Andersen is a Commonwealth-registered civil marriage celebrant and the creative force behind Ink & Vows with Clare. With a unique background as both a former high school teacher and tattoo artist, Clare brings authenticity, creativity and a refreshing edge to every ceremony she creates. Her journey into celebrancy was a natural evolution, combining her passion for connecting with people, her gift for storytelling, and her commitment to helping couples celebrate love on their own terms.

Ink & Vows with Clare specialises in personalised wedding ceremonies designed for couples who refuse to fit into a one-size-fits-all mould. Clare understands that your wedding should reflect who you truly are—whether that means embracing tradition, breaking the rules entirely, or creating something beautifully in between. She works with couples to craft ceremonies that feel authentic, meaningful and unforgettable, infused with the personality, values and quirks that make your love story uniquely yours.

Clare's celebrancy style ranges across the full spectrum of celebrations. She creates stunning traditional ceremonies with all the elegance and grace couples cherish, bold alternative ceremonies that challenge convention, and wonderfully unconventional celebrations that push boundaries. Whether you're planning a romantic beach wedding, an intimate elopement, a themed celebration or a ceremony that breaks all the rules, Clare brings creativity, warmth and just the right amount of edge to your special day.

Beyond her role as a marriage celebrant, Clare also offers professional wedding MC services, bringing her engaging personality and natural charisma to guide your reception with confidence and flair. Her experience as an educator and performer means she knows how to read a room, engage your guests and keep the energy flowing throughout your celebration.

Based in Howard on Queensland's beautiful Fraser Coast, Clare provides wedding celebrant services throughout the region, including Hervey Bay, Maryborough, Bundaberg, Gympie, Rainbow Beach and surrounding areas. She also regularly travels to the Sunshine Coast, Brisbane and Gold Coast, and is available to travel Australia-wide for couples seeking her unique approach to their celebration.

What sets Clare apart is her genuine interest in getting to know you as a couple. She takes time to understand your story, your values, your sense of humour and what matters most to you. This deep understanding translates into ceremonies that feel personal and heartfelt—celebrations that resonate with both you and your guests. Her background in teaching means she's skilled at creating moments of connection and clarity, whilst her artistic sensibility brings visual and emotional depth to every word spoken.

Couples working with Ink & Vows with Clare can expect a collaborative, supportive process from first consultation through to your wedding day. Clare is warm, approachable and genuinely invested in making your celebration everything you've imagined. Whether you're looking for traditional elegance, alternative authenticity or something completely unexpected, she'll help you create a ceremony that tells your story and honours your love.

With Ink & Vows with Clare, you're not just booking a celebrant—you're partnering with someone who believes your wedding should be a true reflection of who you are. Celebrate your love, your way, with the traditions you cherish, the personality that makes you unique, and a little edge that makes it unforgettable.
