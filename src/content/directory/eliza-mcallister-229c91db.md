Eliza McAllister is a wedding celebrant who believes your ceremony should sound like you. Based on Sydney's Northern Beaches, she works with couples across Sydney and beyond—as well as Australia-wide and internationally—to create ceremonies that feel warm, genuine, personal and completely meaningful.

For more than a decade, Eliza has had the privilege of conducting ceremonies for couples from all walks of life. Her journey began simply: helping friends and family create ceremonies that genuinely reflected their relationships. What started through personal connections has grown into a career centred on the moments that matter most—the ones that bring people together.

Eliza's approach is calm, thoughtful and personal. She understands that your ceremony is one of the few parts of your wedding day that is entirely about you as a couple. Rather than delivering a performance, she takes time to get to know you, understand what matters to you, and craft a ceremony that reflects your relationship, your personalities and the way you want to celebrate. The result is something that feels relaxed, authentic and unmistakably yours—with no forced formality or ceremonies that belong to someone else.

Whether you're planning a grand celebration surrounded by loved ones, an intimate elopement with just the two of you, or something beautifully simple, Eliza creates ceremonies tailored to your vision. Her experience spans a diverse range of couples and celebrations, and she brings warmth, organisation and genuine passion to every single one.

From your first conversation through to your wedding day, Eliza provides calm guidance and thoughtful planning to make your ceremony feel simple and enjoyable. She's attentive to the details that matter, and her experience means you can trust the process. Couples consistently describe her as relaxed and friendly, yet thoroughly professional—someone who listens carefully and delivers a ceremony that feels entirely personal.

Based on the beautiful Northern Beaches, Eliza works with couples across Sydney and the surrounding areas, from local venues to relaxed coastal celebrations. She's also available to travel Australia-wide and conduct international destination weddings, bringing her warm, personal approach wherever your celebration takes place.

When couples reflect on their experience working with Eliza, the common thread is clear: she creates ceremonies that feel like them. Personal. Relaxed. Meaningful. Completely their own.
