Michael Janz is an experienced Sydney marriage celebrant based in Randwick, with over a decade of full-time celebrancy experience creating meaningful, memorable wedding ceremonies. Known for his warm, relaxed and professional approach, Michael works closely with couples to design ceremonies that genuinely reflect their personalities, values and unique love story.

Michael believes that a wedding ceremony should be far more than a legal formality—it should be one of the most memorable parts of the day. By combining heartfelt storytelling, sincerity, warmth and just the right amount of humour, he creates ceremonies that are engaging for guests and deeply personal for every couple. His calm guidance, attention to detail and ability to put couples at ease help make the entire process enjoyable and stress-free from the first meeting through to the wedding day.

**What Michael Brings to Your Day**

A ceremony that feels authentic, modern and true to your relationship is at the heart of what Michael delivers. His beautifully written stories capture your personalities and connection, while his reassuring presence guides you from your first chat to your walk back down the aisle. Michael offers seamless, stress-free planning with clear guidance at every step, combined with professional delivery infused with warmth, humour and genuine care.

With extensive local knowledge across Sydney's beaches, parks and landmark venues—from the Eastern Suburbs and North Shore to Sydney Harbour—Michael is equally at home in the Blue Mountains and Southern Highlands. He also travels Australia-wide for couples planning destination weddings.

**Services and Ceremony Options**

Michael specialises in a comprehensive range of ceremonies tailored to your vision and needs. Whether you're planning a fully personalised wedding ceremony that reflects your unique style, an intimate elopement, a micro-wedding, a legals-only marriage, a vow renewal or a naming ceremony, Michael is committed to creating an experience that feels authentic, relaxed and uniquely yours. His duet celebrant option also allows couples to share the honour of officiating with a partner or close friend.

Over the years, he has had the privilege of officiating hundreds of weddings across Sydney and beyond, from intimate elopements and small family gatherings to large celebrations filled with family, friends and cultural traditions. Each ceremony is crafted with the same level of care and attention to detail, ensuring your special day feels personal and unforgettable.

**A Personalised Approach**

Michael is passionate about celebrating love in all its forms and welcomes couples from diverse cultural, religious and family backgrounds. He understands that every couple's story is different, and he takes time to truly understand your relationship, your values and what matters most to you. This thoughtful approach ensures your ceremony feels like a natural extension of who you are as a couple.

Michael commits to booking only one wedding per day, giving you his complete focus, calm support and clear communication throughout the entire planning process. This dedication to quality over quantity means you receive the full attention and care your special day deserves.

**Recognition and Experience**

Michael's commitment to excellence has earned recognition from the Australian Bridal Industry Awards and the Wedding Industry Awards. With more than a decade of full-time celebrancy experience, his goal remains unchanged: to create a wedding ceremony that captures who you are as a couple and leaves you with memories you'll treasure for a lifetime.
